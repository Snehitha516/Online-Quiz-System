Frequently Asked Questions

How is the placement readiness score calculated?

We evaluate your scores from coding mock tests, logical aptitude quizzes, SQL queries performance, resume ATS match rate, and communication during mock interviews to yield a unified index.

Can I export my preparation roadmap?

Yes, once your customized roadmap (30/60/90 days) is generated, you can download a full detailed PDF layout to study offline.

Are the mock interviews live or text-based?

The mock interviews are structured as interactive text exchanges. The AI evaluator scans your responses for key keywords and outputs grades and improvement ideas.
